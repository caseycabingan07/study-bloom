ABOUT THIS HUB

Hi, I'm Casey.

I'm Casey P. Cabingan, a BSIT student at Mabalacat City College. Like many students, I used to feel overwhelmed when several activities, deadlines, and projects piled up at the same time.

Eventually, I started making simple schedules, breaking big tasks into smaller ones, and writing down what I needed to finish. It wasn't a perfect system, but it helped me feel less lost and made schoolwork easier to manage.

I made Study Bloom to share the strategies that helped me—and hopefully remind other students that getting organized doesn't have to be complicated.

📌

My approach

Keep things simple, set realistic goals, and adjust your schedule when life gets busy.

STUDY NOTES

Study smarter, not harder.

A few habits that can make your school days feel more manageable.

📝

Break it down

Turn a big assignment into smaller steps. Starting with one tiny task feels less intimidating.

START SMALL
⏳

Try focus sessions

Work for 25 minutes, then take a short break. Adjust the time to what works for you.

FIND YOUR RHYTHM
📚

Study actively

Test yourself, explain concepts in your own words, or make practice questions instead of only rereading.

LEARN WITH PURPOSE
🌙

Rest counts, too

Sleep and breaks help you recharge. You don't need to be productive every minute of the day.

    STUDENT TOOLKIT

    Useful study habits.

    Simple techniques you can try whenever you need a fresh start.

    🗓️

    Weekly planning

    List deadlines first, then block out study time and breaks around your classes.

    🧠

    Active recall

    Close your notes and try to remember the key ideas. Check what you missed afterward.

    📌

    Prioritize

    Pick the most urgent or important task before spending time on smaller distractions.
